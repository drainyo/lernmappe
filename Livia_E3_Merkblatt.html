Vergleich mit than (als): A cheetah is faster than a lion. Nicht „then“!

2. as … as (Unit 4)

  • gleich: Tom is as old as Ben. (genauso alt wie)
  • nicht gleich: A cat isn't as big as a dog. Horses aren't as heavy as rhinos.
  • Zwischen die zwei as kommt das Adjektiv in der Grundform: as difficult as (nicht „as more difficult as“, nicht „as bigger as“).
  • Das Verb nicht vergessen: is / isn't / are / aren't.

Umformen: A mouse is smaller than a cat. → A cat isn't as small as a mouse.

3. should / shouldn't (Unit 3)

  • Ratschlag: You should + Grundform. You should wear a sweater.
  • Warnung: You shouldn't + Grundform. You shouldn't watch it alone.
  • Kein „to“ danach: nicht „should to wear“. Kein -s: nicht „he shoulds“.
  • Immer den ganzen Satz schreiben, wenn nach „sentences“ gefragt ist.

4. Past simple: negatives and questions (Unit 2)

normale Verbenbe
+She went to the party. He bought a costume.I was at home. We were scared.
−She didn't go. He didn't buy a costume.I wasn't at home. We weren't scared.
?Did you see the film? Where did they go?Was the museum open? Were you scared?
Vergangenheit = did / didn't (für alle Personen). Nicht do/does/doesn't, das ist Gegenwart.
Nach did/didn't kommt die Grundform: didn't buy (nicht bought), did you see (nicht saw). Die Vergangenheit steckt schon im „did“.
