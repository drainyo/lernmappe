Merkregeln
• Present simple: he/she/it + -s (she plays, he watches). Verneinung: don't / doesn't + Grundform. Frage: Do / Does …?
• Past simple: regelmäßig + -ed (visited), unregelmäßig siehe Liste (went, saw).
• Verneinung Vergangenheit: didn't + Grundform (I didn't go). Bei be: wasn't / weren't.
• Frage Vergangenheit: Did + Grundform (Did you go?). Bei be: Was / Were …?
