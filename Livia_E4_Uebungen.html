Day 2 (Di 24.11.): as … as and comparing animals

1. Write the sentences again with (not) as … as. Don't forget the verb!

  1. A dog is bigger than a mouse. → A mouse
  2. A plane is faster than a car. → A car
  3. Lions are more dangerous than cats. → Cats
  4. My sister is older than me. → I
  5. English is easier than maths. → Maths
  6. Anna is 11. Lisa is 11, too. → Anna

2. Look at the table and write sentences.

Day 3 (Mi 25.11.): Past simple negatives and questions

1. Make the sentences negative.

  1. She went to the cinema.
  2. They bought sweets.
  3. He was scared.
  4. We played football.
  5. I saw a ghost.
  6. The children were at home.

2. Write questions.

  1. (you / go / to the party)
  2. (what / you / wear)
  3. (where / she / buy / the mask)
  4. (they / be / scared)
  5. (who / you / meet / at the party)
  6. (the film / be / good)

3. Find the mistake and correct the sentence.

a) He doesn't bought a costume.
b) Did you saw the pumpkin?
c) She didn't went to school.
d) Do you go to the party yesterday?

Day 4 (Do 26.11.): should, vocabulary, writing

1. Give advice. Write full sentences with should or shouldn't.

  1. I'm hungry. (eat something)
  2. It's raining. (forget your umbrella)
  3. My tooth hurts. (go to the dentist)
  4. I've got a test tomorrow. (play computer games all evening)
  5. The dog is very hungry. (give it some food)
  6. It's Halloween night. (go out alone)
