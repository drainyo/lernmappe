H. Musterlösung E-Mail

Hi Sam,
how are you? Last summer I went to Croatia with my dad, my brother Maxi and Clara. We stayed in a small house near the sea. Every day we swam and played volleyball on the beach. One day we took a boat to an island. The weather was great, it was sunny and hot. I was very happy because the holidays were so much fun. What did you do in the summer?
Bye, Livia

I. Musterlösung Picture story

Last Monday Ben woke up late because his alarm clock didn't ring. He ran to the bus stop, but the bus was already gone. So he walked to school. On the way it started to rain and Ben didn't have an umbrella. When he arrived at school, he was very wet. Then his teacher said: "We have a test today!" Ben was not happy. It was a really bad day.
