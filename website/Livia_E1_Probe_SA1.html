1. Reading / 8

Our school trip to the zoo

Last Saturday our class visited the zoo. First we saw the giraffes. They are the tallest animals in the zoo. The biggest one is almost six metres tall! Then we went to the reptile house. My friend Emma didn't want to go in because she is scared of snakes. The anaconda was longer than our classroom, I think.

After lunch we watched the dolphins. Our teacher told us that dolphins are more intelligent than many other animals. They can even understand some signs. The cheetahs didn't move at all. They slept all afternoon, so we didn't see how fast they can run.

My favourite animal was the baby elephant. It wasn't as big as its mum, of course, but it was very cute and funny. On the bus home everybody was tired. It was the best school trip this year!

a) True (T) or false (F)? (4)

  1. The class visited the zoo on Sunday.
  2. Emma didn't go into the reptile house.
  3. The cheetahs ran very fast.
  4. The baby elephant was as big as its mum.

b) Answer the questions. (4)

  1. Which are the tallest animals in the zoo?
  2. Why didn't Emma go into the reptile house?
  3. What did the teacher say about dolphins?
  4. What was the writer's favourite animal?
