1. Reading (8)

b) 5 The giraffes. · 6 Because she is scared of snakes. · 7 Dolphins are more intelligent than many other animals. · 8 The baby elephant.

2. Vocabulary (10)

a) 1 graveyard · 2 mask · 3 pumpkin · 4 sweets · 5 witch (extra: costume)
b) dangerous · heavy · speed · to die out · embarrassed
Rechtschreibfehler: ½ Punkt Abzug pro Wort.

3. Past simple (8)

a) 1 She didn't go to the party. · 2 We weren't embarrassed. · 3 He didn't buy a costume. · 4 I wasn't at home.
b) 5 Did you see the exhibition? · 6 Where did they go last Halloween? · 7 What did she wear to the party? · 8 Was the museum open yesterday?
Typische Fehler: „didn't went“, „didn't bought“ (nach did/didn't kommt die Grundform) und „Did the museum was open?“ (bei was/were kein did).

4. should / shouldn't (6)

1 You should wear a sweater. · 2 You should take it to the vet. · 3 You shouldn't watch it alone. · 4 You shouldn't scare small children. · 5 You shouldn't go to bed so late. · 6 You should do more sport.
Nach should kommt die Grundform ohne „to“: nicht „should to wear“.

5. Comparatives and superlatives (11)

b) 1 faster than · 2 heavier than · 3 more dangerous than · 4 the biggest · 5 the most intelligent · 6 better · 7 the worst
Je Zeile/Satz 1 Punkt. Typische Fehler: „more fast“, „heavyer“, „bigest“ (Doppelkonsonant: big → bigger, biggest), „more better“, „then“ statt „than“.

6. as … as (4)

1 A cat isn't as small as a mouse. · 2 Horses aren't as heavy as rhinos. · 3 English isn't as difficult as maths. · 4 Tom is as old as Ben.
Bei as … as bleibt das Adjektiv in der Grundform: nicht „as smaller as“.

7. Writing (10)

Bewertung (Richtwert): Inhalt, alle Punkte behandelt 3 P · Grammatik, v. a. mindestens zwei Comparatives und ein Superlative richtig 3 P · Wortschatz 2 P · Form und Länge (60–80 Wörter) 2 P.

Beispieltext: My favourite animal is the cheetah. It is yellow with black spots and it is smaller than a lion. Cheetahs live in Africa. They are the fastest animals on land. A cheetah can run faster than a car in the city! It isn't as strong as a lion, but it is more beautiful, I think. I like cheetahs because they are elegant and very fast. One day I want to see one in Africa. (76 words)
